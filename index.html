The Rules

Your Mission

Explore Each Room
Every chamber holds a different kind of puzzle. Read the story and clues carefully — the answers are hidden in plain sight.

Collect Fragments
When you solve a puzzle, you'll receive a Fragment. Each one reveals more about this museum, the people involved, and the dark truth behind the changes to history.

Choose Wisely
At the end, you won't find a single "right" answer. You'll face three different futures. All are valid. None are perfect. Which one will you lock in place forever?
